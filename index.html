Fibi

Fibi is a personal assistant that Dirk built for his own use. It has one user and is not offered to anyone else.

Fibi helps Dirk keep on top of his personal Gmail account and calendar. It sorts incoming email, prepares draft replies for Dirk to review, and adds appointments to his calendar only after he approves them. Fibi cannot send email.

Fibi runs on Dirk's own computer. Read the privacy policy for what it accesses and how that information is handled.
