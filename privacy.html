Fibi privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who this covers

Fibi is a personal assistant used by one person, Dirk, for his own Google account. No other people use it, and it is not available to the public.

What Fibi accesses

  • Gmail, read only: email in the inbox and one newsletter label, to sort it and find what needs action.
  • Gmail, compose: to create draft replies. Drafts stay in Gmail for Dirk to review. Fibi cannot send email; sending is blocked in its code.
  • Google Calendar events: to check appointments, and to add or update an event only after Dirk approves it.

How the information is used

Only to provide these features to Dirk. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone except the service providers below, and only for these features.

Service providers

To judge which emails need attention and to write drafts, Fibi sends the text of an email, capped at about 6,000 characters, to two AI providers: OpenAI and TypeSafe. Their terms for this kind of use state that the content is not used to train their models.

Notifications to Dirk's private Discord channel and his own iPhone app contain an email's sender and subject, never its body.

Where it is stored

On Dirk's own computer, in a local database. Sign-in credentials are kept in the computer's protected credential store. Fibi's stored records are deleted automatically after 90 days. Fibi's activity log never contains email bodies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Fibi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
